START HERE - how to open your website on Windows
==================================================

1. Do NOT open index.html from inside the ZIP file.
   Right-click the ZIP file and choose "Extract All...", then click "Extract".

2. Open the extracted folder called "unique-web".
   You should see index.html, a folder called "assets", and this file.

3. Double-click index.html.
   It should open in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox) and show the
   black hero page with "[Your Full Name]" and the gold "View selected work" button.

If you see plain text instead of the designed page (for example "Menu", "Close",
or a line like "[Skip to content](https://...)"), the file is being shown as text.
Do not open it in Notepad, a text viewer, or a markdown preview. Use the steps
above and open it with your web browser.

Your wallpapers are in the folder assets\wallpapers and are named phase-1.jpg to
phase-8.jpg.

To edit your text, open assets\js\content.js in Notepad. See README.md for more.
